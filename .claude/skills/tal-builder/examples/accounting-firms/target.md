# Target (sample)

A made-up seller, for trying the skill: we sell client-portal and workflow software to independent accounting (CPA) firms in the United States with about 11 to 200 staff, which do tax, audit and advisory work for small and mid-size businesses and families. A good fit is owner-led, has outgrown email and shared drives for collecting client documents, and is growing: hiring accountants, adding an advisory practice, or opening a second office. Wrong, even though they look similar: the national and Big Four networks (too big, and they build their own tools); firms owned by or merged into a larger network; payroll, bookkeeping and accounting software companies (they sell to the same firms rather than being one); venture-backed online bookkeeping startups; and solo practitioners with no staff.
